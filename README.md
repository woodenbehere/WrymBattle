# WrymBattle

**Ember Wyrm**: a 16-bit style Active Time Battle game in the spirit of Final Fantasy VI, built as an installable Progressive Web App.

Play: https://woodenbehere.github.io/WrymBattle/

## The fight

Five heroes take on a dragon in two phases:

| Hero | Class | Special | Limit break |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garrick | Fighter | Rage Strike, Guard | Meteor Cleave |
| Vesh | Thief | Steal, Mug | Shadow Flurry |
| Ilse | White Mage | Cure, Cure All, Protect, Revive | Seraph's Grace |
| Morrow | Black Mage | Flame, Frost, Thunder | Nova |
| Senna | Summoner | Undine, Sylph, Golem | Leviathan |

- **ATB**: each hero's gauge fills by speed; when it is full, their command menu opens. Wait mode pauses gauges while you choose; Active mode does not.
- **Rows**: the back row halves physical damage dealt and taken. Magic ignores rows.
- **Limit breaks**: the pink gauge fills as a hero takes damage (and when an ally falls).
- **World map**: beat the Ember Wyrm and a top-down world map in the style of Final Fantasy II (SNES) opens, with animated water and surf, forests, mountains and deserts. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or press and hold on the map. Visit Ember Peak, Thornwick and Castle Valemont.
- **Random encounters**: Thornhares and Bog Slimes in the grasslands, Grave Wolves in the forest, Sand Rays in the desert, Stone Imps in the hills. Each has its own weaknesses, steals and drops. **Run** escapes most fights, and encounters can be turned off from the map menu.
- **Camp**: rest anywhere on the map to restore all HP and MP and revive fallen heroes. Party HP, MP and items carry over between battles.
- **Walkable towns**: Valemont Village and Thornwick are explorable on foot, with wandering townsfolk to talk to. Walk into a shop door to shop: weapons, armor, items, magic, and the inn.
- **Adventurers’ Guild**: in Valemont Village. Guildmaster Orla and the quest board offer bounties (Goblin Trouble, Wolf Pack, Ogre Bounty and more) that track your kills and pay gil, EXP and gear. Guild rank rises from Copper to Gold and unlocks bigger bounties. Check progress anywhere from the Quests menu.
- **Equipment**: five slots per hero (Weapon, Shield, Head, Body, Accessory) with class restrictions, stat comparisons in the shop, and an Optimize button on the Party screen.
- **Progression**: monsters give EXP and Gil. Heroes start at Lv 10 and grow with class-specific stats up to Lv 60. The magic shop teaches new spells (Cura, Holy, Fira, Blizzara, Thundara, and the Glacia and Raijin summons).
- **Saving**: progress saves automatically in your browser once the Ember Wyrm falls. Start over from the ? panel.
- **Group battles**: fights against up to four enemies, each with its own turn gauge and health bar. Pick a target from the menu (a cursor marks it on screen); summons hit every enemy.
- **Bestiary**: Thornhare, Goblin, Bog Slime, Grave Wolf, Mandrake, Dusk Bat, Sand Ray, Dune Scorpion, Stone Imp, Ash Salamander, the Hill Ogre, and the Vault Sentinel with its Ward Orbs.
- **Frostfang Peak**: a cave in the great mountain range leads into a three-floor dungeon (Frostfang Trail, Icy Ledges, Frozen Summit) with its own monsters: Frost Harpy, Crag Lizard, Snow Wisp, Rime Golem, Mountain Yeti and the Yeti Chieftain. At the top waits Frostfang, the Rime Wyrm: an ice wyrm weak to fire that absorbs ice and unleashes a telegraphed Blizzard.
- **The hidden valley**: deep in the island's great mountain range, ringed by peaks so only the airship can land, lie **Castle Arcanum**, home of the magi, and the village of **Highmere**. New spells (Curaga, Arise, Flare, Meteor, and the summons Odin and Phoenix), new items (X-Potion, Hi-Ether, Megalixir) and crystal-tier gear.
- **The Isle of Aether**: speak with King Corvin and the magi gather in the Circle of Magi to raise a lost island in the northwest. Its winds turn back the airship, so King Aldous's shipwrights build a **Western Port** and a sailing ship. Walk onto the dock to board, sail the open sea, and steer into any shore to land.
- **Tower of Aether**: a hundred generated floors in five tiers (Cloud, Storm, Thunder, Star and Void) with tougher monsters as you climb, Colossus guardians on floors 25, 50 and 75, rare gear every twentieth floor, waystones every tenth floor, and **Zephyrion, the Sky Wyrm** at the top.
- **Status ailments**: monsters can poison (HP drains over time), petrify (the hero is out of the fight until cured; if the whole party turns to stone, the battle is lost) and confuse (the hero attacks at random, sometimes an ally). Antidotes, Gold Needles and Remedies cure them, as do the Heal spell and the monk's Purification. Confusion wears off when a battle ends; poison (which also drains HP as you walk) and stone last until cured, or until you rest at an inn or camp. The Ribbon accessory blocks them all.
- **Magic and items outside battle**: open **Party** on the map or in towns and dungeons to cast healing magic (Cure, Cura, Curaga, Cure All, Heal, Revive, Arise, Sylph, Phoenix), cast Exit inside any dungeon, castle or town, and use potions, ethers, feathers, elixirs and status cures.
- **New magic**: Heal and Exit (warps the party out of any dungeon, castle or town to the world map) at Valemont's magic shop. Ironhold's Rune Hall adds Bio, Firaga, Blizzaga, Thundaga, and the summons Ifrit, Titan and Bahamut.
- **Monk arts and thief tricks**: Ironhold's Training Hall teaches the Fighter Chakra, Pummel, Wave Fist, Earth Slash, Purification and Phoenix Palm (learn three and he becomes a Monk), and the Thief Smoke Bomb, Twin Strike, Venom Edge and Assassinate. Auto battle and habits use all of them.
- **Deepdelve Mine**: sealed by royal order until you finish King Aldous's Hill Ogre quest and defeat Frostfang. Then a mine shaft on the northernmost cape drops five levels (Deepdelve Mine, Crystal Galleries, the Old Sky Road, Fungal Hollow, the Magma Depths) to **Ironhold**, the dwarf city. New monsters: Deep Crawler, Burrow Mole, Crystal Spider, Gloom Shade and Magma Slug. At the bottom of the fifth level coils **Gravemaw, the Earth Wyrm**, which has walled the dwarves in: a full wyrm fight with Rockfall, a petrifying glare and a telegraphed Landslide. It is weak to water and ice and absorbs earth (Titan). Rescue Hilde the crystal-cutter, and read the Sky Folk's tablets along the Old Sky Road.
- **Ironhold**: freed once Gravemaw falls. The **Royal Hall** holds the throne room of King Durgan and Queen Sigrun, the royal bedchamber and a treasury. In gratitude, the King and Queen reveal the secret of the airship. Also in the city: the dwarven forge and armory (Dwarven Axe, Shadow Edge, Runic Staff, Magma Rod, Wyrmbone Whip, Adamant gear), an item shop, an inn, murals, and Elder Thrain's tale of the lost airship. Lifts connect the city to the mine entrance.
- **The airship Aurora**: after the royal audience, Forgemaster Brannoc blasts the old Sky Road open with powder ground from Gravemaw's Earthheart. The Aurora rises to the surface beside the mine. Walk aboard to fly anywhere on the world map, over sea and mountains, with no random battles; choose **Land** to set down on open ground.
- **Save circles**: glowing safe spots in the dungeon that save your progress, allow Tent camping, and are where you wake if the party falls.
- **Mountain side quests**: return Brom the miner's lucky pickaxe, rescue the climber Kessa from behind the Yeti Chieftain, and gather three Frost Lilies for Hermit Oswin's Frost Ward (halves ice damage).
- **Castle grounds**: a stone wall surrounds Castle Valemont and Valemont Village. A clear road runs from the south gate straight to the castle, with the village on both sides.
- **Mini-map**: a map of the world (or the castle, when inside) sits in the top-right corner. Tap it to enlarge.
- **Castle Valemont**: walk inside to meet King Aldous and Queen Mirelle in the throne room, rest in the guest rooms, loot the armory, and earn the Royal Key by slaying the Hill Ogre. The vault is locked, guarded by Captain Brann, and protected by an ancient sentinel.
- **Auto battle**: the Auto button hands any heroes you choose to the AI, using one of four strategies (All-Out Attack, Balanced, Protect Allies, Save MP). Each hero can also get a habit, such as Thief: Always Mug, that overrides the strategy whenever it can.
- **Phase 2**: the Ember Wyrm becomes the Molten Wyrm. Its elemental weaknesses change and it gains row-targeting attacks.

## Install

Open the play link on a phone or in Chrome/Edge, then use **Add to Home Screen** (iOS Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen). After the first load, it works offline. To force the newest version, tap the version number in the footer twice.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game (canvas renderer, battle logic, UI)
- `manifest.webmanifest`: PWA metadata
- `sw.js`: service worker for offline play. When you change files, bump `VERSION` here and `APP_VERSION` (plus the footer button text) in `index.html`.
- `icons/`: app icons

To run locally, serve the folder over HTTP (for example `python3 -m http.server`) and open `http://localhost:8000`. Service workers don't run from `file://`.
