How to play

When a hero's gold gauge fills, their menu opens. Tap any glowing name to switch who acts. Wait pauses the gauges while you choose; Active doesn't.

The back row halves physical damage dealt and taken. Magic ignores rows.

The pink bar is the limit gauge. It fills as a hero takes damage; when full, a Limit command appears.

After the wyrm falls, the world map opens. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or press and hold on the map. Random battles happen as you walk; Camp restores HP and MP, and Run escapes most fights.

Frostfang Peak: a cave in the great mountain range leads up three floors to the summit, where Frostfang, the Rime Wyrm, waits. Glowing save circles are safe spots: they save your progress, let you pitch a Tent, and are where you wake if the party falls. Help the miner, the stranded climber and the hermit on the way up.

Status ailments: some monsters poison (HP drains over time), petrify (the hero is out of the fight until cured) or confuse (the hero attacks at random). Cure them with Antidotes, Gold Needles, Remedies, or the Heal spell. Ailments wear off when a battle ends. A Ribbon blocks them all. Exit warps the party out of a dungeon from the menu. In Ironhold, the Rune Hall sells Firaga, Bio and the summons Ifrit, Titan and Bahamut, and the Training Hall teaches the Fighter monk arts (learn three to become a Monk) and the Thief new tricks.

Deepdelve Mine: on the northern cape, a mine shaft drops five levels to Ironhold, the dwarf city. Read the old tablets on the Sky Road, slay the Magma Worm, and the dwarves can blast free the airship Aurora. Walk aboard to fly anywhere on the world map with no random battles; choose Land to set down on open ground.

Castle Valemont: walk in to meet the King and Queen, rest in the guest rooms, raid the armory, and earn your way into the royal vault. Walk into people to talk to them.

Auto hands chosen heroes to the AI with a strategy you pick.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to choose, Esc to go back.

Towns: walk into Valemont Village (inside the castle walls, on both sides of the road) or Thornwick. Walk around, talk to townsfolk, and walk into shop doors to shop or rest at the inn. The Adventurers’ Guild in Valemont Village posts bounties for gil, EXP and gear. Monsters give EXP and Gil, and heroes level up as they fight. Progress saves automatically once the wyrm falls.

Heroes on auto · habit

A habit overrides the strategy whenever it can be used, using the strongest version of a spell the hero has learned. New spells appear here once bought. Full limit breaks still fire.
