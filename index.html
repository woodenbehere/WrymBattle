How to play

When a hero's gold gauge fills, their menu opens. Tap any glowing name to switch who acts. Wait pauses the gauges while you choose; Active doesn't.

The back row halves physical damage dealt and taken. Magic ignores rows.

The pink bar is the limit gauge. It fills as a hero takes damage; when full, a Limit command appears.

After the wyrm falls, the world map opens. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or press and hold on the map. Random battles happen as you walk; Camp restores HP and MP, and Run escapes most fights.

Auto hands chosen heroes to the AI with a strategy you pick.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to choose, Esc to go back.
